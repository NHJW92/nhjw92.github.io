And it's already day 5!

Three more days have gone by since the last post, and a lot has happened again!

On Wednesday afternoon we first went to Tanah Lot. The ride with the Grab driver was insane, and we were both glad we survived it. I was offered a helmet, which I put on reluctantly, because, well, ew, lice. But afterwards Niels was jealous that I had a helmet and he didn't. Tanah Lot is a temple that stands on a rock in the sea. We got the idea from (of course) asking Chat, and at first we didn't realise it's one of the most touristy spots around Canggu. But that was understandable, because holy moly, it was beautiful!

We started with a nice cup of coffee, made possible by: Tim, thank you! This was a very special cup of coffee, though. Bali has luwaks, animals that look a bit like a cross between a marten and a cat. Their natural diet includes coffee cherries. That's why you can order luwak coffee on Bali: the luwak's droppings are collected, and the coffee beans are then cleaned and dried. Because the beans have passed through the luwak's digestive system, they've gone through a kind of fermentation. Well, it's all very nice as an idea, and the coffee was definitely good, but Niels and I aren't exactly such coffee connoisseurs that we actually tasted any difference. 😂

After that we had 45 minutes to walk to the fire dance (a 10-minute walk), but we only got in one minute before it started. That was because the views were so beautiful and we wanted to take photos of everything. So in the end we made it to the fire dance just in time, and it was a bit of a letdown for us. We'd expected a bit more spectacle, but at least we got a cool selfie with one of the dancers.

Then we went to eat at the café, but apparently nobody else needed food, because at 7 pm we were still the only ones there eating. We got eaten alive by mosquitoes, so we wanted to leave quickly. That turned out to be a bit harder than expected, because there were no Grab drivers around anymore. Luckily a taxi driver saw us standing there completely lost and took us with him. During that ride we learned a few more Balinese words, which turns out to be a real life hack, because every local instantly likes you 10x more when you say 'Om Swastiastu' (a Balinese greeting that wishes you peace and greetings from God).

Once home we went to bed, so we could start fresh the next morning. Unfortunately things turned out very differently for me, because at 4 am my Bali belly kicked in (maybe that's also why nobody was eating at Tanah Lot). So I spent the whole next day in bed, apart from a few trips to the bathroom, and Niels chilled at our Airbnb.

The day after that (Friday) I thought I was already feeling better, and we set off to buy fake Salomons, since we didn't have hiking shoes yet. We took a Grab (a car this time, because no way were we sitting on a Grab scooter for 40 minutes in Canggu again) and drove 40 minutes to a store I'd seen on TikTok (not knowing there was apparently also one five minutes' walk from our Airbnb).

We both scored nice Salomons there. Both white, because they'll end up black anyway. Then I wasn't feeling so great after all, so we went back home. We had lunch and then walked to the beach to chill at a beach club.

That was absolutely amazing! We could swim in the sea, swim in the pools, chill on the loungers and test whether a cocktail helps against Bali belly (no, you just get drunk really fast). We had pizza there and watched the sunset. We ended the evening with a wonderful Balinese massage.

This morning we packed our things to move on to our next stop. For the next 7 days we're staying in an Airbnb in the jungle, and as a plant lover I think it's absolutely amazing. All my houseplants in real life, but 10,000x bigger and so many more! Some of the insects make one hell of a racket, but hey, that's part of it. The Airbnb is gorgeous! We look out over the jungle and have 3 private baths, a foot bath, an outdoor kitchen and an outdoor bathroom too.

From here we want to do more day trips around Bali, so be sure to come back later to read about all the fun things we've done!

As it starts to rain a little here, which only makes the jungle more beautiful, I say “Om Swastiastu” and see you laterrrr!
