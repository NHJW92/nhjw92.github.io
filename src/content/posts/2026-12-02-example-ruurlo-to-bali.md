This is an **example post** to show how a trip appears on the site. Edit it once the real journey has happened, or delete it.

The route on the map has four points:

1. **Ruurlo**: home, where the trip starts.
2. **Amsterdam Schiphol**: by train via Zutphen.
3. **Abu Dhabi**: the first flight, with a transfer at Zayed International.
4. **Denpasar**: the second flight, and the first stop with a story (this post).

Ruurlo, Schiphol and Abu Dhabi are *stops*. They're on the route but have no story of their own. They live in `src/data/route.yml`, or under **Route stops** in Pages CMS. Denpasar is a *story*: any post with coordinates is added to the route automatically.

Each point records how you got there (train, flight, boat, scooter…). The map draws flights as dotted arcs, boats as dashed lines and overland legs as solid lines.

> The dates are placeholders. Set them to the real departure day so the route stays in the right order.
