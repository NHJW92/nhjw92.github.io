Aaaand we are off! We got dropped off by babets parents at schiphol, drank our last coffee in the netherlands at the gate and started off our journey. 

The first flight went by quite quickly, we had enough entertainment with us but sleeping was not really happening as it was noisy and uncomfortable. We came to the conclusion that sleeping as little as possible might help us to skip the jetlag as we landed in denpasar at 21:30 and thus could go to bed at the end of our journey at a normal local time. This would mean though that we would have to go 33 hours without sleeping. 

We landed in Abu Dhabi after that first 6 hours and met a nice girl called Tessa and got a coffee there as well to hopefully survive the next flight as good as the first one. 

The second flight was a lot tougher as we were more tired and the entertainment options were used up and we found ourselves powernapping for about 30 minutes which helped a lot with how we felt. The flight took 9 hours and we landed in Denpasar and got through immigration with no problems thanks to Babets perfect preparation.

We also had a driver arranged and he brought us to the apartment. At this point we just dropped everything took a shower and went to sleep to recover from the flight. 

Our first day we woke up at 10:30 and we checked out a hip breakfast place. The service there was amazing, we got cold towels to cool our faces and clean our hands which was nice because our bodies were coping with the heat and humidity with a lot of sweating.

We then went to the beach where we got ourselves 2 sunbeds to relax. We stayed there until the evening watching surfers and sipping from a coconut or our bintang beers. I also went to the nearby boost juice place and got us some smoothies. Boost juice is a smoothie franchise that I know from my earlier travels in Australia and its still as good as back in the days.

In the evening we went to a Warung to eat which is a local restaurant which makes indonesian food at low prices. We paid 3€ for the two of us for rice, babi pangang and chicken saté with some vegetables and a fried egg. The food was a bit spicy but very enjoyable and for that price I cannot complain. How the Warung works was a bit of a challenge for Babet though as it was a bit like the muur in the netherlands and you had to tell a lady at the other side what you wanted while 3 other people were also screaming their order so it was quite chaotic and you had to make decisions on the spot about what you wanted to eat. I have never experienced a more hectic way of ordering food so next time we will be mentally prepared better as we know what is coming now.

The next day, today we went to another hip breakfast place where we paid 13€ total for our meal so there is quite a big difference here between hip western food prices and local indonesian cuisine. But we expected this as Canggu is the most touristy part of indonesia probably. We then got some nice cookies and I bought some fake birkenstock sandals before going home. The morning was nice and sunny so we chilled by the pool trying to get our blog working and writing this story. 

While writing this story the first real downpour of the rainy season hit us so now we sit at our apartment balcony and enjoy the cosy feeling that the rain brings.
