Hello for another story that I will tell you today! Currently we are sitting in our hostel, its called tipsea turtle - and it lives up to its name, drinking a virgin pina colada and a mojito and reflecting on our last few days on gili t. When we arrived on the island we checked in to the hostel and immediately enjoyed the company that hostels bring with them. Its our first hostel that we visit and its a nice change of scenery to also have some other people to connect to. On the first day we met two scottish girls in the pool and due to the two happy hours our hostel has from 12-15 and 17-20 o`clock we got a bit drunk and went on a night out with the hostel. The party was fun but the island is a bit empty due to it being low season and thus so was the club. We also met a girl called Alma at the hostel. Shes from sweden and had quite some interesting party tricks that she would show off. One of them was holding a beer between heer boobs and then tried to pour it into someones mouth. 

The next day we went to the MantaDive shop next to the harbour and we walked in saying we wanted to do the openwater course. We heard from other people at the hostel that this was the best diving shop on gili t and that coincided with the advice my father gave me on what diving schools i should go with as they are certified with SSI. There are basically two big organizations schooling people in scubadiving, one is called SSI and the other PADI. My dad has done all of his schooling with SSI so he might be a bit biased but on the other hand he is the one that already has all of the diving experience and more than 100 dives so I took that advice into consideration. We had to fill out some forms, download the app and fill in our medical information, pay and then we were good to go starting the next day with our introduction theory and an introductory dive in the afternoon. After that we went to the beach to have a beach day for the rest of the day but while walking there Babet fell on a gravel road and we stopped at a different beachclub than we had initially planned. They were very helpful disinfecting the wound and then we stayed there for the rest of the day and had some lunch before we went home again. In the evening we ate some pizza at our hostel and went to bed quite early because we had to wake up early as we would meet at 8:30 at the diveshop.

The next 3 days would look quite similar, first day was theory plus learning techniques in the pool, so learning about the buoyancy jacket, how the air goes from the tank to everywhere needed and how we can clean our goggles when they fill up with water. And in the afternoon our first dive, I will include one divelog in the pictures so you can see what the conditions around here are. We saw a little reefshark hiding under a rock, some clownfish(nemo), and 3 turtles and thousands of other fish that I cant identify. The reef here is very beautiful and the first dive spot was called Halik(shallow) as we dived up to 18m which we will be doing with all of our openwater dives. Next morning we did more theory and techniques and then in the afternoon another dive. And on our final day two more dives focused on applying the techniques we learned in the Ocean but before that also always a little bit of fun and looking at all the cool marine life. We saw more turtles nemos and a gigantic barracuda, patrick star, and some sea cucumbers which were surprisingly large although everything looks about 25% bigger underwater due to how light behaves in a liquid. We went to Halik a second time, then after to Turbo and later Turtle point gili meno. Diving with the instructors we had was so much fun, we decided to go out with them tonight and that we also want to do the advanced course with them to get more used to diving and to get more freedom for future dives. 

After getting our openwater diving license we took a day off because it was quite exhausting, learning new things everyday from 8-17, then coming home and also doing the online theory course after because you need to take an online exam. We went to the beach and Babet wanted to snorkel so she rented snorkel gear. While she did put on sunscreen, she still got burned very bad on her backside, probably because she snorkeled for quite a while. The triggerfish she encountered actually saved her from an even worse sunburn as there were a lot, enough to scare her out of the water after one of them attacked her as it was protecting its territory. It did not hit her though as Babet learned how to defend herself with her fins. Also her scratches from that first day in gili are not healing super fast because we went into the ocean every day since so now we are giving that some rest to hopefully heal back up before we do the advanced openwater course.
